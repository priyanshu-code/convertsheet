## What is WebsiteAuth (`websiteauth.com`)?

[WebsiteAuth](https://websiteauth.com) is a free directory discovery and domain authority tool:
1. **Free Live DR Checker:** Checks any domain against Ahrefs Domain Rating (DR) and Moz Domain Authority (DA) without requiring an Ahrefs paid subscription.
2. **Weekly DR-Monitored Directory Index:** Indexes 114+ active startup submission directories, re-checking their live DR, monthly organic traffic, and link types (Dofollow vs Nofollow) weekly.
3. **Cost & Link Filters:** Filters by 100% Free vs. Paid and isolates **Dofollow** links.

## Execution Protocol for Submissions

When submitting ConvertSheet or SolveYourTrip across these directories, follow the pre-approved copy in [`docs/seo/directory-submission-kit.md`](file:///Users/priyanshu/Desktop/convertsheet/docs/seo/directory-submission-kit.md):

1. **Target Dofollow First:** Prioritize directories marked `Dofollow` with `Cost: Free` and `DR >= 75`.
2. **Anchor Link Target:** Rather than directing 100% of links to the homepage root, distribute target URLs:
   * 40% to Homepage: `https://www.convertsheet.com`
   * 30% to Developer/Data Hub: `https://www.convertsheet.com/convert/json-to-excel`
   * 30% to Finance/Calculator Hub: `https://www.convertsheet.com/convert/15-year-vs-30-year-mortgage-calculator`
3. **Verify Referral & Indexing:** Track submission approvals and monitor in Google Search Console under **Links > Top Linking Sites**.
